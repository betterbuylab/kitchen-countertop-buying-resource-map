# Buyer-fit checklist

- [ ] How much counter depth can you permanently allocate?
- [ ] Are you cooking for one, a couple, or a family batch routine?
- [ ] Is frozen snack reheating more common than full meal prep?
- [ ] Do you need a combo oven to replace a toaster and small oven?
- [ ] Will smoothie or frozen-drink routines need ice-crushing power?
- [ ] Is dishwasher-safe cleanup a hard requirement?
- [ ] Do dual baskets matter for mixed meals with different finish times?
- [ ] Are you comparing wattage marketing against real basket size?
