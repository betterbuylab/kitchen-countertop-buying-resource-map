## Buyer-fit questions

1. How much counter depth can you permanently allocate?
2. Are you cooking for one, a couple, or a family batch routine?
3. Is frozen snack reheating more common than full meal prep?
4. Do you need a combo oven to replace a toaster and small oven?
5. Will smoothie or frozen-drink routines need ice-crushing power?
6. Is dishwasher-safe cleanup a hard requirement?
7. Do dual baskets matter for mixed meals with different finish times?
8. Are you comparing wattage marketing against real basket size?
